    How this is chosen

    The chart shows every institute worldwide on one scale. Bars show adopted or announced funding for the whole institute in a funding year that overlaps , the calendar year of the data date. Where several years qualify, the one containing the data date is used, if there is one. Proposals, one-off amounts and other years get no bar; institutes with only such funding are listed below the bars with their Budget value. Components are excluded. Period means are labelled. EUR figures are rough conversions at the ECB's annual-average rates and do not compare like with like.
