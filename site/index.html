    Funding and staff

    How this is chosen

    Budget bars share one scale and show adopted or announced funding for the whole institute in a funding year that overlaps , the calendar year of the data date. Where several years qualify, the one containing the data date is used, if there is one. Proposals, one-off amounts and other years keep their amounts in the records. Components are excluded from the comparison. Period means are named in the figure explanations and the records. EUR figures are rough conversions at the ECB's annual-average rates and do not compare like with like. Staff bars have their own scale and show published totals with dates. Dates, bounds and commitments are explained on hover, focus or tap on a figure; the same explanation is available to screen readers. Bounds and approximations also stay on the number as up to, ~ or +. Technical counts, targets and ranges remain in the records and do not become total bars.
